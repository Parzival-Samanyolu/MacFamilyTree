# Reports

*Reports* turns your tree into readable documents. Choose a report, the person it is about, how many generations, the language and a privacy level, then press **Create report**. The preview shows exactly what you will share.

| Report | Contents |
|---|---|
| Individual summary | Narrative paragraphs plus a table of events and facts |
| Ancestor report | Ahnentafel (Sosa-Stradonitz) numbered, grouped by generation |
| Descendant report | d'Aboville numbered (1, 1.1, 1.1.2 …), grouped by generation |
| Family group sheet | Both partners, the marriage and a table of children |
| Family book | Ancestors and descendants in one document with continuous footnotes and one index |
| Bibliography | Every source, Chicago-style, with how many citations use it |

**Narrative.** Sentences are written from templates, for example *“John Smith was born on 3 March 1850 in Konya. He married Mary Jones on 12 June 1874. They had 2 children: William (1875), Anna (1878).”* Dates read naturally for their precision (*on 3 March 1850*, *in March 1850*, *around 1850*, *between 1880 and 1890*). Turkish reports use correct vowel harmony and no gendered pronouns (*Konya'da*, *Kars'ta*, *İzmir'de*). Children who share the surname are listed by given name only.

**Edit the wording.** *Sentence templates* lists every sentence in English and Turkish. Use placeholders such as `{name}`, `{He}`, `{date}`, `{place}`, `{spouse}`, `{children}`, `{n}`. Leave a field empty to return to the built-in sentence.

**Citations** become numbered footnotes (author, *title* (publication), page). The same source cited twice shares a number.

**Privacy.** *Mask* shows living people as “Living” with no dates, places or notes; *Remove* leaves them out entirely (counts of children adjust). Nobody's private details appear in anyone else's narrative.

**Output.** Download **HTML** (self-contained, prints well, includes a table of contents and name index) or **Markdown**, or **Print** to save a PDF. Reports are generated from your data and shown in a sandboxed preview that cannot run scripts.
