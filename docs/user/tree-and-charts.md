# Tree and charts

## Family tree
Pick a person, then open **Family tree**.
* **Chart type** – hourglass (ancestors above, descendants below), ancestors, or descendants. Set generations with the `Up`/`Down` steppers. Direction: top-to-bottom or left-to-right. *Show spouses* includes partners; a person with several partners shows them all.
* **Navigate** – drag to pan, mouse-wheel/pinch (or `+`/`−`) to zoom, `0` or *Fit* to fit, click the minimap to jump. Arrow keys move the selection between people; `Enter` edits; `R` makes the selected person the root.
* **Context menu** (right-click): edit, set as root, collapse/expand a branch, add child/partner, copy name.
* **Repeated ancestors** (pedigree collapse) appear once in full and again as a dashed stub marked ↺; *Jump to first occurrence* finds the original. Dashed lines mark adopted, foster and step links. `…` on a card means more relatives lie beyond the generation limit or a collapsed branch.
* **Card style** – card size, dates, birth place, colour by sex / generation / your own colour labels, rounded corners. Save named styles to reuse.
* **Export** – SVG, PNG, or Print (use the browser/system dialog to save a PDF).

## Fan chart
Ancestors as rings (full circle, half circle or 90°) with 2–8 generations, coloured by branch, sex or generation. Click a slot to select that person; `Shift`+click opens the editor. Export as SVG or print.

## Relationship calculator
Choose two people. The result names the relationship ("second cousin once removed", Turkish *amca kızı*, …), shows every route and common ancestors (half-relations are labelled), the coefficient of relationship, and each person's inbreeding coefficient. Terminology is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Russian and Arabic.
