# Sources, repositories and tasks

**Sources & tasks** has four tabs.

* **Sources** – add and edit sources. Pick a *source type* (census, birth/marriage/death record, church book, newspaper, website, book, interview) to get a title hint for that type, and rate its reliability (unreliable → primary/original). Link a repository; the table shows how many citations use each source. Cite a source from a person's *Notes & sources* tab (or from an event).
* **Repositories** – the archives, libraries and websites that hold your sources.
* **To-do list** – research tasks with priority and due date, optionally linked to a person. Completed tasks are hidden unless you ask for them.
* **Unsourced facts** – every birth, death and marriage that has no citation yet: your list of what to verify next. Citing a fact removes it from the list.

Deleting a source also removes its citations, and can be undone.
