# Timeline and calendar

**Timeline** lists events in date order for the whole tree or for the selected person and their families. *Show historical events* adds a built-in overlay of world events that fall within your tree's era (you can replace the list with your own in the project). The **Life spans** tab draws one bar per person; living people run to today, and bars with an estimated end (no death date) are paler. Filter by surname; click a bar to open the person.

**Calendar** shows a month at a time: birthdays of living people (with the age they turn), wedding anniversaries of couples who are both alive, and death anniversaries. *Include people who have died* adds their birthdays and anniversaries too. **Export iCalendar** writes a `.ics` file with yearly-repeating events that opens in any calendar app. Only living people's birthdays are exported unless you include deceased people.
