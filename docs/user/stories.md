# Stories

**Stories** lets you write a narrative around your research.

1. *New story*, then give it a title.
2. Add blocks: **Heading**, **Text** (blank line = new paragraph), **Quote**, **Person write-up** (the generated life narrative and event table for someone), **Picture** (from your Media library, with a caption) and **Timeline** (a person's dated events).
3. Use ↑ / ↓ to reorder and ✕ to remove a block. Changes save automatically.
4. The preview updates as you type; pick English or Türkçe and whether living people are shown, masked or left out (their pictures are omitted too).
5. Export as **HTML** (one self-contained file, pictures included) or **Markdown**.
